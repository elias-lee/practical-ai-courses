## The running case study: the SitRep Assistant

Throughout both courses we work on one realistic problem: turning messy field reports — emails,
notes, PDFs — into a clear, structured **situation report (SitRep)** for decision-makers.

- In **AI Literacy**, you *use* and *evaluate* the SitRep Assistant through a chat tool.
- In **AI Engineering**, you *build* it — one architectural layer per class.

All organizations, places and figures in the case study are **fictional**.

## How to use this site

- Every class page has **learning objectives**, **"Know the Term" sidebars** (click to expand),
  a **Prompt Lab** with copyable prompts, and a **Check Your Understanding** quiz with instant
  feedback.
- Use the **search** box at the top — it works offline.
- Click **Mark class complete** at the end of a class; your progress is saved in this browser
  and shown on the course pages.
- New terms? See the [Glossary](glossary.md). Lost in the big picture? See the [AI Map](ai-map.md).
