# About these courses

**Practical AI** is an independent learning resource: two 8-class courses, AI Literacy for
non-technical learners and AI Engineering for technical ones. It is not affiliated with, and does
not represent the views of, any organization, company or AI provider mentioned in it.

## Data

All organizations, places, people and figures in examples and in the SitRep case study are
**fictional**, or taken from public sources where stated.

Never paste confidential, personal or sensitive data into an AI tool unless your organization has
explicitly approved that tool for that kind of data.

## Time-sensitive content

AI changes quickly. Model names, product features, laws and framework recommendations are marked
**"as of 2026"**. Concepts change far more slowly, and they are the focus of these courses:
computational thinking, prompts, context, harnesses, loops, evaluation and security.

## Using the site offline

The site is also published as a zip file on the project's Releases page. Unzip it anywhere and
open `index.html` in a web browser. No internet connection or installation is needed. Search,
quizzes and progress tracking all work offline, and progress is stored only in your own browser.

## Editions

- **Learner edition** (this website): everything learners need.
- **Instructor edition** (zip on the Releases page): adds facilitation guides with timings, demo
  scripts, common misconceptions and quiz answer rationale.
