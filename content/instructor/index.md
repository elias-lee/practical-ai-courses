# Instructor Guide

This section appears only in the **instructor edition**. It contains facilitation guides for each
class: timing plans, live-demo scripts, common misconceptions and quiz answer rationale.

## Standard session plan (~3 hours)

| Time | Block | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 10 min | Warm-up quiz | 3 questions from the previous class's quiz; ask for a show of hands on each option before revealing |
| 30 min | Concept | Lead with the analogy; keep slides minimal — the class page *is* the handout |
| 20 min | Live demo | Always include one **deliberate failure** and fix it live |
| 60 min | Lab | Pairs; circulate; collect 2–3 good examples to share |
| 20 min | Check Your Understanding | Learners answer on the page; discuss the most-missed question, focusing on *why the wrong answers are tempting* |
| 10 min | Exit ticket | "One thing I'll try at work this week" |

## Ground rules to state in every class

- Use only your organization's **approved** AI tools, and only with data they are approved for.
- The case study data is fictional. Do not substitute real sensitive data during labs.
