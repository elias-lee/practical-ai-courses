??? info "Know the Term: Diffusion models"
    **Diffusion models** are the main technology behind AI image and video generation. During
    training, the model sees millions of images gradually buried in random noise and learns to
    reverse the process, removing a little noise at a time. To create a new image, it starts from
    pure noise and removes noise step by step, guided by a text description such as "a flooded
    village road at dawn". They work differently from the language models in this course, which
    predict text one token at a time.

    **Analogy:** a sculptor who starts with a rough block and removes a little material with each
    pass until the figure described in the brief appears.

    **Why you care:** generated images can look like real photographs. Never use a generated image
    where readers could take it as evidence of a real event, label AI-generated visuals clearly,
    and check your organisation's policy before using them in public communications.
