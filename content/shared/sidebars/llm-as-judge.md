??? info "Know the Term: LLM as a judge"
    **LLM as a judge** means using one AI model to grade the output of another against written
    criteria, a **rubric** such as "every figure has a source; conflicting reports are flagged;
    neutral tone". It is used for qualities that simple rules cannot check, and it is much
    cheaper than having people grade thousands of answers. But the judge has biases of its own:
    it tends to prefer longer answers, confident wording, the first option it is shown, and text
    written in its own style. So its grades are only trusted after being compared with grades
    from people on the same examples.

    **Analogy:** an assistant marker for exams. Useful for getting through the pile, but the chief
    examiner checks a sample of their marking before the results are released.

    **Why you care:** an automated grade is only as good as its rubric and its calibration. If a
    vendor reports "95% quality", ask who or what did the grading, and how often it agreed with
    humans.
