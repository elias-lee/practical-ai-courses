??? info "Know the Term: Human in the loop"
    **Human in the loop** means a person reviews, corrects or approves an AI system's work at
    agreed points before it has real effects: before a report is sent, a payment is made, a record
    is changed or a decision about a person is taken. The design question is not "human or AI?"
    but *where* the human checkpoints sit and *what* the person actually checks. A reviewer who
    clicks "approve" on everything without reading is not a real checkpoint, just a signature on
    the AI's work.

    **Analogy:** a senior officer who clears a press release before it goes out. The drafter does
    most of the work, but nothing leaves the building without that clearance.

    **Why you care:** you remain accountable for what goes out under your name. Decide in advance
    which steps need your approval, and give yourself enough time to check them properly.
