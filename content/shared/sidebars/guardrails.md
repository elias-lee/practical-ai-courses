??? info "Know the Term: Guardrails"
    **Guardrails** are checks placed around an AI model that stop unwanted input going in or
    unwanted output coming out. They are ordinary software rules, not requests to the model:
    blocking a tool the assistant is not allowed to use, removing phone numbers and email
    addresses before text is stored, refusing requests that are outside the system's purpose, or
    checking that a report has the required headings before anyone sees it. Some guardrails are
    simple pattern checks; others use a second, smaller model as a classifier.

    **Analogy:** the barriers on a mountain road. They do not steer the car, but they stop a bad
    moment from becoming a disaster.

    **Why you care:** "we told the AI not to" is not a control. When you ask how an AI system is
    kept safe, ask which guardrails are enforced in code, and what happens when one is triggered.
