??? info "Know the Term: Multimodal models"
    A **multimodal** model can work with more than one kind of input or output ("modality"):
    text, images, audio, video, or documents such as PDFs and scanned forms. It turns each kind
    of input into the same internal form (tokens and embeddings), so it can, for example, read
    a photo of a handwritten distribution list, describe a chart, transcribe a voice note, or
    answer a spoken question aloud. Most leading chat tools are multimodal (as of 2026), though
    which modalities are switched on depends on your organization's tool and settings.

    **Analogy:** a colleague who can read the report, look at the photos attached to it and
    listen to the voicemail, rather than one who only reads typed text.

    **Why you care:** photos, scans and recordings from the field can now go straight into the
    AI tool. But reading an image is another place for errors (a misread "7" for "1"), and
    images and recordings can contain faces, voices and locations that need the same data
    protection as text.
