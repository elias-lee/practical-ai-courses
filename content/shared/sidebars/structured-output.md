??? info "Know the Term: Structured output"
    **Structured output** means asking the AI to answer in a fixed, predictable shape instead of
    free-flowing text: a table with named columns, a form with set headings, or a data format
    such as **JSON** (a plain-text format that computer programs can read, made of labelled
    fields like `"district": "Aramu"`). You describe the shape, meaning the fields, their order
    and what to write when something is missing, and the AI fills it in. Software that uses AI
    goes a step further: it can require the model to follow an exact template and reject any
    answer that does not fit.

    **Analogy:** handing someone a printed form to fill in instead of a blank sheet of paper.
    Every answer lands in the same box, every time.

    **Why you care:** structured answers are faster to check, easy to compare across reports
    and ready to paste into a spreadsheet or another system. An empty box also shows you
    straight away what information is missing.
