??? info "Know the Term: AI governance"
    **AI governance** is the set of laws, policies, standards and oversight bodies that shape how
    AI is built and used. It works at several levels. **Organizations** set rules on approved
    tools, data and accountability. **National and regional laws** set obligations, such as the
    European Union's **AI Act**, which sorts AI uses into risk tiers with stricter rules for
    higher-risk uses. **International** efforts build shared understanding and cooperation,
    including the **OECD AI Principles** (2019, updated 2024), UNESCO's **Recommendation on the
    Ethics of AI** (2021), the **Global Digital Compact** (adopted by UN Member States in 2024),
    and the UN's **Independent International Scientific Panel on AI** and **Global Dialogue on AI
    Governance** (both established in 2025). **Standards** such as the NIST AI Risk Management
    Framework (2023) and ISO/IEC 42001 (2023) turn principles into practice. The landscape is
    changing quickly (as of 2026).

    **Analogy:** road safety, which combines car-design standards, national driving laws, local
    speed limits and your own organization's rules for its vehicles.

    **Why you care:** governance decides which tools you may use, for which data and with what
    checks. When unsure, your organization's policy is the rule that applies to you first.
