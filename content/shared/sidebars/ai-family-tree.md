??? info "Know the Term: The AI family tree"
    **Artificial intelligence (AI)** is the broad field of making computers do tasks that
    normally need human intelligence, such as recognising speech or planning a route. **Machine
    learning (ML)** is one branch of AI: instead of following rules a person wrote, the system
    learns patterns from examples. **Deep learning** is a kind of machine learning that uses
    large **neural networks** with many layers, and it drives most AI progress since around 2012.
    **Generative AI** is deep learning that *creates* new content (text, images, audio, code)
    rather than only sorting or predicting. **Large language models (LLMs)**, the engines inside
    chat tools, are generative AI for text. Each circle sits inside the one before it:
    AI ⊃ machine learning ⊃ deep learning ⊃ generative AI.

    **Analogy:** "vehicle" ⊃ "car" ⊃ "electric car" ⊃ "self-driving electric car". Every
    self-driving electric car is a vehicle, but most vehicles are not self-driving electric cars.

    **Why you care:** when someone says "we use AI", ask which kind. A spam filter, a satellite
    flood map and a chatbot are all "AI", but they fail in very different ways and need
    different checks.
