??? info "Know the Term: Zero-shot vs few-shot prompting"
    A **zero-shot** prompt asks the AI to do a task with instructions only and no examples: *"Write
    a one-line summary of this report."* A **few-shot** prompt adds a handful of worked examples
    (the "shots") of the input and the output you want, then gives the new input. The model spots
    the pattern in your examples, such as the length, tone, structure and level of detail, and
    copies it. Two or three good examples often do more than a paragraph of description, because
    style is easier to show than to explain.

    **Analogy:** handing a new colleague two past donor letters and saying "one like these",
    instead of writing a page of style guidance.

    **Why you care:** when the AI keeps getting the tone or layout "almost right", examples are
    usually the quickest fix. Just make sure they are good ones, because the AI will copy their
    flaws too.
