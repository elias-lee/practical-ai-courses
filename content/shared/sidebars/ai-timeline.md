??? info "Know the Term: A short AI timeline"
    **1950:** Alan Turing asks "Can machines think?" and proposes his imitation game.
    **1956:** the term "artificial intelligence" is coined at a workshop at Dartmouth College.
    **1970s–1980s:** **expert systems** encode specialists' knowledge as thousands of
    hand-written "if… then…" rules; they work in narrow areas but are costly to maintain, and
    funding cools in periods later called "AI winters". **1990s–2000s:** **machine learning**
    takes over, learning patterns from data for tasks like spam filtering. **2012:** a deep
    neural network wins the ImageNet image-recognition contest by a wide margin, starting the
    **deep learning** boom. **2017:** Google researchers publish the **transformer** design.
    **2018–2020:** large language models such as GPT-2 and GPT-3 appear. **November 2022:**
    ChatGPT's release brings generative AI to the general public. **2023–2026:** models become
    **multimodal** (text, images, audio), **reasoning models** appear, and **agents** that can
    take actions with tools spread into workplace products (as of 2026).

    **Analogy:** like the history of flight: decades of gliders and false starts, then a few
    breakthroughs close together that suddenly made air travel ordinary.

    **Why you care:** AI has been "about to change everything" several times before. Knowing
    the history helps you tell a real shift from a sales pitch.
