??? info "Know the Term: Energy use of AI"
    AI runs in **data centres** that use electricity to power chips and water or air to cool them.
    Energy is spent twice: once to **train** a model (a large, one-off cost) and again every time
    it is **used** (called **inference**), which adds up across billions of requests a day. A
    single short text answer uses a small amount of energy, but long documents, image and video
    generation, reasoning models that "think" at length and agents that make many calls per task
    use much more. The International Energy Agency estimated in 2025 that data centres used about
    1.5% of the world's electricity in 2024 and projected this could roughly double by 2030, with
    AI a major driver. Estimates vary widely and change quickly (as of 2026).

    **Analogy:** a single car trip is minor, but traffic across a whole city every day adds up to
    a real footprint, and a truck costs more per trip than a scooter.

    **Why you care:** use AI where it adds real value, choose a smaller or simpler tool when it is
    enough, and avoid regenerating the same thing over and over for no reason.
