??? info "Know the Term: System prompt"
    A **system prompt** is a set of standing instructions given to the AI before the conversation
    starts. It usually sets the AI's role, its rules and its style, such as *"You are an assistant
    for humanitarian staff. Answer in plain English. Never invent figures."* The people who set up
    the tool write it, and you usually cannot see it. Many chat tools also let you write your own
    version, often called **custom instructions**, a **project**, or a custom assistant, that
    applies to every chat in that space. Your prompt is added on top of it.

    **Analogy:** the standing orders a new colleague receives in their induction pack. Each
    request you make is a new task, but the standing orders still apply.

    **Why you care:** if you retype the same background ("I work in programme support, write in
    British English…") in every chat, put it in custom instructions once. It also explains why
    the same question can get different answers in different tools.
