??? info "Know the Term: AI agent"
    An **AI agent** is a language model that works in a **loop** instead of answering once. It is
    given a goal and a set of **tools** (search a database, read a file, send a draft for
    review). At each turn it looks at what has happened so far, decides the next action, uses a
    tool, reads the result, and repeats — until the goal is met, a limit is reached, or a person
    stops it. A plain chatbot *answers*; an agent *acts, checks and continues*.

    **Analogy:** the difference between asking a colleague a question and delegating a task to
    them — once delegated, they decide the next step themselves and come back when done.

    **Why you care:** agents can finish multi-step jobs on their own, but every extra step is
    another chance to go wrong. Good agents come with clear limits, and a human who approves the
    steps that matter.
