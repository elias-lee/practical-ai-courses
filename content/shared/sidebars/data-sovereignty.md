??? info "Know the Term: Data sovereignty"
    **Data sovereignty** is the idea that data is subject to the laws and control of the place it
    comes from, or of the people or community it describes. For AI tools, it raises practical
    questions: *Where* are my prompts and files processed and stored? *Which country's* laws apply
    to them? *Who* can access them, including the tool's provider? Could they be used to train a
    model? Your organization's own data-protection policy and legal obligations (for example the
    EU's GDPR) add further conditions, and governments and communities may have strong
    expectations about data collected in their territory. Answers depend on the tool, the contract
    and the configuration, not on the brand name.

    **Analogy:** sending a sealed parcel abroad. Once it crosses a border, the rules of the
    country it is sitting in may apply, whatever you wrote on the label.

    **Why you care:** before a tool is approved for sensitive data, someone must check where that
    data goes and who controls it. That is why "approved for this data classification" matters.
