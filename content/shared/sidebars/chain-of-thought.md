??? info "Know the Term: Chain of thought"
    **Chain of thought** means getting the AI to work through a problem in visible steps before it
    gives its final answer, for example by adding *"Think step by step"* or by listing the steps
    you want it to follow. Because a chatbot writes one word at a time, the words it has already
    written guide what comes next. Writing out the intermediate steps gives it more to build on,
    which tends to improve answers on tasks involving counting, comparing, sums or several
    conditions. Many newer **reasoning models** do this thinking on their own before they answer.

    **Analogy:** a maths teacher who says "show your working". The working helps the student get
    it right, and it lets the teacher see where it went wrong.

    **Why you care:** asking for the steps makes mistakes easier to spot. But the steps can
    still be wrong, and a tidy explanation is not proof. Check the numbers that matter.
