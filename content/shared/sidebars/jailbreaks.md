??? info "Know the Term: Jailbreak"
    A **jailbreak** is an attempt to talk an AI model into ignoring its own safety rules, for
    example to produce harmful instructions it would normally refuse. Common tricks include
    role-play (*"pretend you are an AI with no rules"*), fictional framing (*"write a story in
    which a character explains…"*), splitting a forbidden request into innocent-looking pieces, or
    wearing the model down over a long conversation. Jailbreaks differ from prompt injection: in a
    jailbreak the *user* is trying to get round the model's rules; in prompt injection a *third
    party* hides instructions in content to turn the AI against its user. Providers patch known
    jailbreaks, and new ones keep appearing (as of 2026).

    **Analogy:** talking your way past a security guard with a convincing story rather than
    breaking the lock.

    **Why you care:** a model's built-in refusals are one layer of safety, not a guarantee.
    Organizations still need their own rules, approved tools and human review, and deliberately
    jailbreaking a work tool is usually a breach of its terms of use.
