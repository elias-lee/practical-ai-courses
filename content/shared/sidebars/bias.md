??? info "Know the Term: Bias in AI"
    **Bias** in AI means the system's outputs are systematically skewed for or against certain
    groups, places, languages or viewpoints. It usually comes from the **training data**: models
    learn from huge amounts of text, most of it from the internet, which over-represents some
    languages (especially English), regions and voices and under-represents others. Bias can also
    come from how a system is designed, or from the examples and instructions it is given. It
    shows up as stereotypes in generated text, weaker answers in less-represented languages,
    assumptions about who holds which job, or some communities being quietly left out of a
    summary.

    **Analogy:** a map drawn only from travellers' reports. The busy roads are detailed; the
    places few travellers visit are blank or wrong, and the map doesn't tell you which is which.

    **Why you care:** bias is rarely obvious in a single answer. Look for who is missing or
    described differently, compare outputs across groups and languages, and don't let AI make
    judgements about people on its own.
