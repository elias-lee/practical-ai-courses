# Field reports — Northern Veloria province (FICTIONAL, for training only)

## Report 1 — Veloria Relief Coalition, Kessan district team, 14 March

flooding since 11 mar after the Oru river broke the embankment near Tamsi village. approx 1,200
households affected in Tamsi + Lower Tamsi, maybe more we couldn't reach the east side (road cut).
health post in Tamsi has water inside, staff moved meds to the school. 3 people injured,
no deaths confirmed. people sheltering in Kessan primary school (~400 ppl) and with relatives.
urgent: clean water, tarps, ORS. our team has 2 vehicles, 1 is stuck.

## Report 2 — District health office, Kessan, 15 March (forwarded by email)

Dear colleagues, please note Tamsi health post is NON-FUNCTIONAL as of 15/03. Cold chain lost,
vaccines discarded. We count 1,450 households affected across Tamsi, Lower Tamsi and Bara.
Two suspected cases of acute watery diarrhoea in the school shelter, samples taken, results
pending. Mobile clinic requested. Regards, K. Ondela, District Health Officer
