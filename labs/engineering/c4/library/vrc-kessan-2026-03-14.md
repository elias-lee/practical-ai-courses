# Flood rapid assessment, Kessan district
date: 2026-03-14
lang: en
source: Veloria Relief Coalition, Kessan district team

Flooding began on 11 March after the Oru river broke the embankment near Tamsi village. The
team estimates approximately 1,200 households affected in Tamsi and Lower Tamsi. The east bank
could not be assessed, so the real figure may be higher. Three people were injured and no deaths
have been confirmed.

About 400 people are sheltering in Kessan primary school and others are staying with relatives.
The school has no separate latrines for women and girls. Priority needs reported by the
community are clean drinking water, tarpaulins and oral rehydration salts. The team has two
vehicles; one is stuck in mud north of Tamsi.
