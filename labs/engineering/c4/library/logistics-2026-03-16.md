# Access and logistics update, Northern Veloria
date: 2026-03-16
lang: en
source: Northern Veloria logistics working group

The bridge between Bara and Kessan collapsed on 13 March. Trucks cannot cross the Oru river
at this point. The only open route for heavy vehicles is a 40 km detour through Aramu
district, which adds about three hours to each convoy. The detour road is unpaved and
becomes impassable after heavy rain.

Light vehicles reach Lower Tamsi only on foot or by motorbike from the north. A convoy of four
trucks with tarpaulins and water purification tablets left the Aramu warehouse on 16 March.
Fuel stocks at the Aramu warehouse are sufficient for about ten days of operations.
