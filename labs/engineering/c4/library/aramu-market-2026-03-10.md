# Market monitoring, Aramu town
date: 2026-03-10
lang: en
source: Aramu district food security office

Prices in the Aramu town market were stable in the first week of March. Maize flour sells at
420 dinars per kilogram and cooking oil at 1,100 dinars per litre. Traders in town report
good supplies of rice and beans from the south.

Supplies of fresh vegetables into town fell slightly because of rain on the market road.
Traders expect supplies to recover within two weeks. Aramu town remains the main trading hub
for Kessan and Tamsi.
