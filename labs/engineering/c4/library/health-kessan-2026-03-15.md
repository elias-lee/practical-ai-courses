# Health bulletin, Kessan district health office
date: 2026-03-15
lang: en
source: District health office, Kessan

The Tamsi health post has been non-functional since 15 March. Flood water entered the
building, the cold chain was lost and all vaccines stored there were discarded. Staff moved
essential medicines to Kessan primary school.

The office counts 1,450 households affected across Tamsi, Lower Tamsi and Bara. Two suspected
cases of acute watery diarrhoea were identified among people in the school shelter; samples
were taken and results are pending. The office has requested a mobile clinic and a
replacement vaccine refrigerator. Measles vaccination planned for 20 March is postponed.
