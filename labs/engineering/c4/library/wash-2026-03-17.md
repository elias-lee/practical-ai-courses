# WASH rapid assessment, Tamsi and Bara
date: 2026-03-17
lang: en
source: Veloria Relief Coalition, water, sanitation and hygiene team

Water from the Bara well is visibly turbid and tested positive for faecal contamination. The
team began chlorinating the well on 17 March and distributed household water filters to 150
families. Residents are advised to boil drinking water until further notice.

At Kessan primary school, the shelter has four latrines for about 400 people, well below the
minimum standard. Two additional latrines, with separate facilities for women and girls, are
under construction. Hygiene kits are needed for at least 300 households.
